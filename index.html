WHO WE ARE

About NSS

Serving Society, Building Students

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a student community service programme that encourages young people to participate in social and community development activities.

Our NSS unit focuses on creating awareness, helping communities and developing leadership, teamwork and social responsibility among students.
